How I work

Here’s how an engagement usually takes shape.

I usually begin with positioning and messaging, then spend my time wherever your team needs it most.

  1. 01

    Story and foundation

    Positioning, narrative, messaging, and category development, captured in a blueprint your whole team can work from.

  2. 02

    Go-to-market plan

    How you’ll go to market: the ICP, segments, channels, and a marketing plan that supports the sales number.

  3. 03

    Campaigns that support sales

    Integrated campaigns, content, and sales tools that carry the story into market and give sales air cover.

  4. 04

    Team and engine

    Making sure the team and agencies are set up to keep it all running, including help with hiring.

All of it is aimed at the numbers you’re accountable for: qualified pipeline, shorter sales cycles, and revenue.

AI is embedded in how I work. I use AI agents and structured workflows across every part of an engagement, so I can deliver senior-led depth at the pace you need to move.
